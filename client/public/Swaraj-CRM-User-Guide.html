Swaraj Dealer CRM — User Guide

How Super Admin, Head Office, Area Manager and Dealer work together.

Open CRM

1. Idea of the product

Swaraj Dealer CRM is the internal tool for the dealer network. Head office sees the full country picture. Area managers run their own dealers. Dealers upload shop/event media. Super Admin controls branding and dealer posters made from Excel lists.

हिन्दी: यह सिस्टम डीलर नेटवर्क चलाने के लिए है. सुपर एडमिन पूरा कंट्रोल, हेड ऑफिस फील्ड ऑपरेशन, एरिया मैनेजर अपने डीलर, डीलर अपनी दुकान की फोटो/वीडियो.

2. Four roles

Super AdminHighest access. HQ users, letterhead (header/footer on pictures), dealer posters, everything Head Office can do.
Head Office AdminArea managers, all dealers, visits, media, reports, audit, settings. Can also create posters from AM sheets.
Area ManagerOnly their dealers. Create/edit dealers, log visits, approve dealer uploads, send Excel lists, approve posters made from their Excel.
DealerOwn profile, upload photos/videos, see approved posts, visit history. Cannot see other dealers or other areas.

Login: choose the role card (no password screen). Session stays on refresh; it does not jump to another profile.

3. How people move through the app

Dealer uploads media → Area Manager approves / rejects → Approved posts are visible Area Manager uploads Excel of dealers → Super Admin / HO opens that sheet only → Maps columns + picture → 8K posters → Sends for approval to THE SAME Area Manager → That AM approves / rejects → Never sent to another AM

4. Super Admin panel

  1. Dashboard — totals, dealers per AM chart, HQ users, activity.
  2. Header / Footer — upload a full picture, drag texts, fonts, colours, sizes. Download HD/8K PNG. This layout is used on bulk posters.
  3. Dealer posters — inbox of Excel files from area managers. Open a sheet → map Name / Address / Phone / Website → Create posters → confirm → progress bar. Then Send to this area manager for approval (confirm). Edit / delete / bulk delete with confirm. Full preview with previous/next.
  4. HQ Users — create Head Office / Super Admin logins.
  5. Also: Area Managers, Dealers (all India), Visits, Posts & Media, Reports (tables + charts), Audit logs, Settings.

5. Head Office Admin panel

  1. Dashboard: area managers, dealers, pending media, visits, state chart, visit trend.
  2. Create and manage Area Managers and Dealers; assign a dealer to an AM (confirm first).
  3. Visits for the network. Media: HQ posts go live; they can also approve.
  4. Same dealer-poster inbox as Super Admin if they open Dealer posters.
  5. Reports export CSV. Delete actions always ask confirmation.

6. Area Manager panel

  1. My dealers — add/edit dealers in their area only.
  2. Visits — record field visits.
  3. Approve uploads — only dealer media from their dealers. Confirm before approve/reject.
  4. Excel & posters — upload .xlsx / .xls / .csv (confirm). Super Admin parses it. When HQ sends posters back, they appear here for approval. AM never sees another area’s posters.
  5. AM does not design posters and does not upload marketing posts (dealers upload; AM approves).
Excel tip: keep a header row with columns like Name, Address, Phone Number, Website. Extra title rows above the table are skipped.

7. Dealer panel

  1. Dashboard: own stats, Area Manager contact, recent uploads and visits.
  2. Profile: shop details.
  3. Upload video/photo from shop, mela, demo — goes to AM as pending.
  4. Approved posts: only live content. Visit history: AM visits to this dealer.

8. Important rules

RuleWhy
Posters follow the Excel ownerThe AM who uploaded the sheet is the only AM who can approve those posters.
Confirm before share / deleteCreate posters, send to AM, Excel send, assign dealer, delete, approve/reject.
English / हिन्दी switchTop bar. Choice is remembered.
Do not mix roles on one browser sessionLog out, then pick another role.

9. छोटा हिंदी सार

  • डीलर फोटो/वीडियो भेजता है → एरिया मैनेजर अप्रूव करता है.
  • एरिया मैनेजर Excel भेजता है → सुपर एडमिन पोस्टर बनाता है → उसी एरिया मैनेजर को अप्रूवल.
  • दूसरे क्षेत्र का पोस्टर किसी और AM को नहीं जाता.
  • कुछ भी शेयर/डिलीट से पहले कन्फर्म बॉक्स आता है.
